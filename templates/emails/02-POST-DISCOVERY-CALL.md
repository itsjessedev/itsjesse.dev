# Email Template: Post-Discovery Call Follow-Up

**Use when:** After the initial discovery call
**Send within:** Same day or next morning
**Goal:** Recap conversation, outline next steps, set expectations for proposal

---

## Subject Line

- Great chatting - next steps for [PROJECT NAME]
- [PROJECT NAME] - recap and next steps
- Following up on our call

---

## Template

```
Hi [NAME],

Great talking with you today about [PROJECT].

Here's a quick recap of what we discussed:

**The Problem:**
[1-2 sentences summarizing their challenge]

**The Solution:**
[1-2 sentences summarizing proposed approach]

**Key Requirements:**
- [Requirement 1]
- [Requirement 2]
- [Requirement 3]

**Next Steps:**
I'll put together a detailed proposal including scope, timeline, and investment. You can expect that by [DATE - typically 2-3 business days].

In the meantime, if you could send over:
- [Any materials you need - logos, access, docs, etc.]
- [Additional info if needed]

That would help me finalize the details.

Let me know if I missed anything from our conversation.

Talk soon,
Jesse
```

---

## Variation: If You're Not the Right Fit

```
Hi [NAME],

Thanks for taking the time to chat today about [PROJECT].

After learning more about what you need, I don't think I'm the best fit for this one. [BRIEF REASON - e.g., "This would require ongoing maintenance that's outside my current availability" or "This is more of a design project than development."]

That said, you might want to check out:
- [Resource or referral if you have one]
- [Alternative approach suggestion]

Best of luck with the project, and feel free to reach out if you have something else in the future.

Best,
Jesse
```

---

## Notes

- Send the same day while conversation is fresh
- Recap shows you listened and understood
- Giving a proposal deadline sets expectations
- Asking for materials keeps momentum going
