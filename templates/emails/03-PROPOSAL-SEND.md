# Email Template: Sending the Proposal

**Use when:** Delivering the proposal to the client
**Send:** When proposal is ready (within promised timeframe)
**Goal:** Present proposal, highlight key points, prompt decision

---

## Subject Line

- Your [PROJECT NAME] proposal is ready
- Proposal: [PROJECT NAME] - [THEIR COMPANY]
- [PROJECT NAME] - scope, timeline & investment

---

## Template

```
Hi [NAME],

As promised, here's the proposal for [PROJECT NAME].

**Quick Summary:**
- **Timeline:** [X weeks]
- **Investment:** $[AMOUNT]
- **Deliverables:** [Key deliverable 1], [Key deliverable 2], [Key deliverable 3]

I've attached the full proposal with all the details. The key sections are:

1. **Proposed Solution** (p. 2) - What I'll build and how it addresses your needs
2. **Deliverables** (p. 3) - Exactly what you'll receive
3. **Timeline** (p. 4) - Week-by-week breakdown
4. **Investment** (p. 5) - Pricing and payment schedule

**To move forward:**
1. Review the proposal
2. Sign the attached Service Agreement
3. Submit the deposit

This proposal is valid for 30 days.

Let me know if you have any questions or would like to discuss anything. Happy to hop on a quick call if that's easier.

Best,
Jesse

---
Attachments:
- [PROJECT NAME] Proposal.pdf
- Service Agreement.pdf
```

---

## Variation: Following Up on Sent Proposal

**Wait 3-5 business days before following up**

```
Subject: Checking in - [PROJECT NAME] proposal

Hi [NAME],

Just wanted to check in on the proposal I sent over [X days ago].

Have you had a chance to review it? I'm happy to answer any questions or adjust anything that doesn't quite fit.

If timing has changed or this isn't the right fit, no worries at all - just let me know either way.

Best,
Jesse
```

---

## Notes

- Keep the email short - the proposal has the details
- Highlight the 3 things they care most about
- Make the next steps crystal clear
- Include deadline to create gentle urgency
- Follow up once, then let it go
