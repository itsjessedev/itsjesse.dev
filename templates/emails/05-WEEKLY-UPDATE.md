# Email Template: Weekly Progress Update

**Use when:** Regular weekly check-in during project
**Send:** Same day each week (e.g., every Friday)
**Goal:** Keep client informed, maintain trust, surface any blockers

---

## Subject Line

- [PROJECT NAME] - Week [X] Update
- Progress update: [PROJECT NAME]
- [PROJECT NAME] update - [DATE]

---

## Template

```
Hi [NAME],

Here's your weekly update on [PROJECT NAME].

---

**COMPLETED THIS WEEK:**
- [Task 1]
- [Task 2]
- [Task 3]

**IN PROGRESS:**
- [Current task] - [% complete or brief status]

**NEXT WEEK:**
- [Planned task 1]
- [Planned task 2]

---

**TIMELINE STATUS:** [On Track / Slightly Behind / Ahead]
[If behind: Brief explanation and plan to get back on track]

**BLOCKERS:** [None / Description of any blockers]
[If blockers exist: What you need from them or how you're resolving it]

---

**DEMO / PREVIEW:**
[Link to staging site, screenshot, or video if applicable]

---

Let me know if you have any questions or feedback.

Talk next week,
Jesse
```

---

## Variation: Milestone Completed

```
Hi [NAME],

Big update - [MILESTONE NAME] is complete!

**What's Done:**
[Brief description of completed milestone and what it means]

**What You Can See:**
[Link to demo, staging site, or screenshots]

**What's Next:**
Moving into [NEXT PHASE]. Key focus for the coming week:
- [Task 1]
- [Task 2]

We're [on track / ahead of schedule] for the [DATE] delivery.

Take a look when you get a chance and let me know any feedback.

Jesse
```

---

## Variation: Need Client Input

```
Hi [NAME],

Quick update on [PROJECT NAME] - making good progress, but I need your input on something.

**Status:** [Brief current status]

**Decision Needed:**
[Clear description of what you need them to decide]

Options:
1. [Option A] - [Brief pro/con]
2. [Option B] - [Brief pro/con]

My recommendation: [Your recommendation and why]

Let me know by [DATE] so I can keep moving. Happy to jump on a call if you'd like to discuss.

Jesse
```

---

## Notes

- Send on the same day every week (consistency builds trust)
- Keep it scannable - bullets and headers
- Be honest about timeline status - surprises kill trust
- Include something visual when possible (screenshots, demos)
- If you need something from them, make it clear and give a deadline
