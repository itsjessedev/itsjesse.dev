# Email Template: Initial Inquiry Response

**Use when:** Responding to a new project inquiry from the contact form
**Send within:** 24 hours of receiving inquiry
**Goal:** Acknowledge, show interest, schedule discovery call

---

## Subject Line Options

- Re: [Their subject] - Let's discuss your project
- Thanks for reaching out - next steps
- [PROJECT TYPE] project - I'd love to learn more

---

## Template

```
Hi [NAME],

Thanks for reaching out about [PROJECT TYPE / BRIEF DESCRIPTION].

[PERSONALIZED SENTENCE ABOUT THEIR PROJECT - show you read their message]

I'd love to learn more about what you're looking to build. A quick call would help me understand your needs and give you a clear picture of how I can help.

Would any of these times work for a 20-30 minute call?

- [DAY], [DATE] at [TIME]
- [DAY], [DATE] at [TIME]
- [DAY], [DATE] at [TIME]

If none of those work, feel free to grab a time that does: [CALENDLY LINK - if you have one]

In the meantime, you can see examples of similar work I've done here: https://itsjesse.dev/projects

Looking forward to hearing more about your project.

Best,
Jesse
```

---

## Variations

### If they included detailed requirements:

```
Hi [NAME],

Thanks for the detailed overview of [PROJECT]. This sounds like an interesting project, and I think I can help.

Based on what you've described, this seems like it would fall into my [SERVICE CATEGORY] work. I've done similar projects like [RELEVANT PROJECT] that had [SIMILAR CHALLENGE].

Before I put together a proposal, I'd like to hop on a quick call to make sure I fully understand your needs. Would [DATE/TIME] or [DATE/TIME] work for you?

Best,
Jesse
```

### If it's vague/needs more info:

```
Hi [NAME],

Thanks for reaching out! I'd love to help with [WHAT THEY MENTIONED].

To make sure I can give you the most useful information, could you tell me a bit more about:

- What problem are you trying to solve?
- What tools/platforms are you currently using?
- Do you have a target timeline or budget in mind?

Once I have a better picture, I can let you know if this is something I can help with and what it might look like.

Best,
Jesse
```

---

## Notes

- Respond within 24 hours (faster = better impression)
- Always personalize - reference something specific from their message
- Keep it short - save details for the call
- Include a clear call-to-action (schedule the call)
