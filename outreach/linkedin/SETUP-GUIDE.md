"Currently working on: [project type]

### Posting Schedule
- **Mon/Wed/Fri** or **Tue/Thu** - pick a rhythm
- Best times: 8-9am or 12pm (when people check LinkedIn)
- Consistency beats frequency

## Warm Outreach Process

### Step 1: Identify Targets
- Business owners in industries you serve
- People posting about tech problems
- People who engage with relevant content

### Step 2: Warm Them Up (3-5 days)
- Like 2-3 of their posts
- Leave one thoughtful comment (not "Great post!")
- Engage genuinely, no pitch

### Step 3: Connect
Connection request message (optional, keep short):
"Hey [Name], been enjoying your posts about [topic]. Would love to connect."

### Step 4: After They Accept (wait 1-2 days)
DM:
"Thanks for connecting! Saw your post about [specific thing].

I actually help businesses with [related service]. Not pitching anything - just curious, is [problem] something you're actively trying to solve?"

### Step 5: If They Engage
Move to call: "Would you be open to a quick 15-min call to see if I could help?"

## LinkedIn DOs and DON'Ts

### DO
- Be helpful in comments
- Share real experiences
- Respond to every comment on your posts
- DM people who engage with your content
- Post consistently (even if 1x/week)

### DON'T
- Pitch in connection requests
- Send walls of text in DMs
- Only post about yourself/services
- Ignore comments
- Be salesy in every post
