# Cold Email Templates

## The Golden Rules
1. **Short** - 3-5 sentences max
2. **Personal** - Reference something specific about THEIR business
3. **Value-first** - Lead with their problem, not your services
4. **Clear CTA** - One simple ask (usually a quick call)
5. **Follow up** - Most deals close on follow-up 2-4

---

## Template 1: The Specific Problem

**Subject:** Quick question about [specific thing you noticed]

I noticed [specific observation about their business - bad website, manual process, etc.].

I help businesses like yours [solve specific problem] - recently helped [similar business type] [specific result].

Would you be open to a 15-min call to see if I could help?

## Follow-Up Sequence

### Follow-up 1 (3-4 days later)
**Subject:** Re: [original subject]

Hi [Name],

Just floating this back up. I know you're busy.

If [specific problem] isn't a priority right now, no worries - just let me know and I'll stop bugging you.

Jesse

### Follow-up 2 (1 week later)
**Subject:** Re: [original subject]

Hi [Name],

Last note from me. If you ever need help with [service], I'm here.

Feel free to reach out whenever - jesse@itsjesse.dev

Jesse

---

## What NOT to Do

- Don't send walls of text
- Don't list all your services
- Don't use "I hope this email finds you well"
- Don't attach anything in first email
- Don't be vague - specificity = credibility
- Don't give up after one email
